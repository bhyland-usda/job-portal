Acceptable Use Policy

All users of the USDA employee portal must read and accept this policy before continuing.

This portal is a U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) information system provided for authorized use only. By using this system you agree to the terms below.

1. Authorized Use

This system is intended for official USDA business and approved professional networking among employees. You must access only the data and functions for which you are authorized.

2. Acceptable Conduct

  • Keep your account credentials confidential and never share them.
  • Treat colleagues with respect; harassment, discrimination, and abusive content are prohibited.
  • Post accurate information and respect the intellectual property of others.

3. Prohibited Activities

  • Sharing classified, Personally Identifiable Information (PII), or other sensitive data outside approved channels.
  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to any account, system, or data.
  • Using the system for commercial solicitation, partisan political activity, or unlawful purposes.

4. Monitoring & Privacy

Use of this system constitutes consent to monitoring, recording, and auditing. There is no expectation of privacy for activity on this system.

5. Consequences

Violations may result in loss of access, disciplinary action, and civil or criminal penalties under applicable law.
