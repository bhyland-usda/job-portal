Export My Data

Download a copy of the personal data this site holds about you. The export is a single JSON file containing the information associated with your account.

The export includes:

  • Profile — your account details (name, email, headline, about, location). Your password is never included.
  • Posts — posts you have published to the feed.
  • Comments — comments you have left on posts.
  • Connections — connection requests you have sent or received.
  • Kudos — kudos you have given and received.
  • Accomplishments — accomplishments recorded on your profile.
Download my data (JSON)

The file is generated on demand and downloaded directly to your device. It may contain sensitive information, so store it somewhere safe.
